Cherry Jubilee

JUMP TO RECIPE

Recipe Tips For Cherry Jubilee

  • Cherries: While you can use frozen sweet cherries if you're preparing this dish out of season, fresh cherries offer the best flavor and texture.

  • Easy pitting: Pit the cherries using a cherry pitter, or press the cherries through a metal pastry tip or wide straw to remove the pits quickly and easily.

  • Alcohol: Swap the bourbon for brandy or dark rum, or omit for an alcohol-free version.

  • Heavy skillet: Use a heavy-bottomed skillet to prevent the sugary sauce from scorching.

  • Avoid overcooking: Make sure to cook the cherries just until softened to keep them from becoming mushy.

  • Balance the sweetness: If the cherries are naturally sweet, you can adjust the sugar quantity as needed.

  • Serve warm: Make sure to serve the cherries warm, straight from the pan, for the perfect contrast of temperatures with vanilla ice cream.

Directions

Step 1

Stir together sugar, orange zest and juice, lemon juice, vanilla, and 1/4 cup of the water in a large stainless steel skillet. Cook over medium-high, stirring often, until simmering and sugar is dissolved, about 1 minute.

Step 2

Add cherries and salt; cover and cook until cherries release juices, about 2 minutes. Uncover and continue to cook, stirring occasionally, until cherries begin to soften, about 3 minutes.

steps 3

Remove from heat and add bourbon; return to heat and cook over medium, swirling pan frequently and spooning sugar mixture over cherries, until raw alcohol aroma mostly dissipates and cherries are soft, about 3 minutes.

Step 4

Stir together cornstarch and remaining 1 tablespoon water in a small bowl until combined; add to cherry mixture, and continue cooking until sauce thickens, stirring often, about 1 minute.

Ingredient

  • 1/2 cup granulated sugar
  • 1 tsp. grated orange zest, plus 1/4 cup fresh juice (from 1 medium orange)
  • 2 tsp. fresh lemon juice (from 1 small lemon)
  • 1 tsp. vanilla extract
  • 1/4 cup, plus 1 Tbsp. tap water, divided
  • 1 lb. fresh sweet cherries, pitted
  • 1/8 tsp. kosher salt
  • 3 Tbsp. bourbon
  • 1 tsp. cornstarch
  • Vanilla ice cream, for serving
